<p>Pakistan has some of the greatest trekking in the world and very few of the crowds. Five of the world's fourteen 8,000-metre peaks are here, along with the longest glaciers outside the polar regions. These are the treks we would choose for 2027, from the classic three-week glacier routes to shorter walks that suit a first visit.</p>
<h2><strong>The great glacier treks</strong></h2>
<h3><strong><a href="/trekking/k2-base-camp-trek/">K2 Base Camp Trek</a></strong></h3>
<p><em>21 days, demanding.</em> The most famous trek in Pakistan: up the Baltoro Glacier to Concordia and on to the foot of K2 at about 5,100 metres. No pass and no technical climbing, but two weeks of camping on rough moraine. Season late June to early September.</p>
<h3><strong><a href="/trekking/gondogoro-la-circuit-trek/">K2 Base Camp and Gondogoro La Trek</a></strong></h3>
<p><em>21 days, strenuous.</em> The same route to Concordia and K2, then over the Gondogoro La at 5,585 metres on fixed ropes into the Hushe valley. The finest single viewpoint in the Karakoram. Season mid-July to late August.</p>
<h3><strong><a href="/trekking/biafo-hispar-trek/">Biafo Hispar Snow Lake Trek</a></strong></h3>
<p><em>22 days, strenuous.</em> A traverse of 116 kilometres of connected ice over the Hispar La, crossing Snow Lake on the way. Far emptier than the Baltoro, with roped glacier travel on the upper sections. Season July and August.</p>
<h2><strong>Shorter treks for a first visit</strong></h2>
<h3><strong><a href="/trekking/nangma-valley-trek/">Nangma Valley Trek</a></strong></h3>
<p><em>10 days, easy.</em> A granite valley above Kanday village in the Hushe region, with big walls on every side and summer pastures to camp in.</p>
<h3><strong><a href="/trekking/fairy-meadows-base-camp-trek/">Nanga Parbat Base Camp Trek via Fairy Meadows</a></strong></h3>
<p><em>10 days, moderate.</em> From Fairy Meadows at about 3,300 metres to Beyal Camp and the base camp area under the Raikot face of Nanga Parbat. The jeep road to Fairy Meadows is open from about May to October.</p>
<h3><strong><a href="/trekking/rakaposhi-base-camp-trek/">Rakaposhi Base Camp Trek</a></strong></h3>
<p><em>7 days, moderate.</em> From Minapin in the Nagar valley up beside the glacier to the base camp meadows under Rakaposhi, 7,788 metres.</p>
<h3><strong><a href="/trekking/thalle-la-trek/">Thalle La Trek</a></strong></h3>
<p><em>9 days, easy.</em> A crossing between the Shigar and Khaplu valleys through alpine pastures.</p>
<h2><strong>Higher and quieter</strong></h2>
<h3><strong><a href="/trekking/rush-lake-trek/">Rush Lake Trek</a></strong></h3>
<p><em>14 days, moderate.</em> A Nagar valley trek to one of the highest lakes in the region, with glacier walking and wide views of the Karakoram.</p>
<h3><strong><a href="/trekking/majrooh-top-trek/">Majrooh Top Trek</a></strong></h3>
<p><em>10 days, easy.</em> A newer, non-technical route near Skardu, explored by our own team.</p>
<h3><strong><a href="/trekking/khosar-gang-peak/">Khosar Gang Peak Climb</a></strong></h3>
<p><em>12 days, moderate.</em> A trekking peak in the Shigar valley and a first step from trekking towards climbing.</p>
<h2><strong>How to choose</strong></h2>
<p>If you have three weeks and solid multi-day trekking behind you, choose between the K2 base camp trek and the Gondogoro La. Our <a href="/trekking/k2-treks/">K2 treks</a> page compares the two side by side. If this is your first trek at altitude, start with Nangma, Rakaposhi or Fairy Meadows.
