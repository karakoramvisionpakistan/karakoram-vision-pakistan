<h2><strong>The short answer</strong></h2>
<p>Our scheduled <a href="/trekking/k2-base-camp-trek/">K2 Base Camp trek</a> departures start from <strong>USD 2,000 per person for groups of 10 or more</strong>, for the full 21-day programme from Islamabad. We also run the trek for smaller groups and on private dates; those are priced individually, so <a href="/contact-us/">ask us for a quote</a> with your group size and preferred dates.</p>
<h2><strong>What the price usually covers</strong></h2>
<p>Every quote is written out in full before you book, but a K2 base camp trek with us normally includes:</p>
<ul>
<li>Trekking permit, national park fees and registration in Skardu</li>
<li>A licensed guide from the region for the whole trek</li>
<li>Cook and kitchen crew, with all meals on the trek</li>
<li>Porters, paid at the official Gilgit-Baltistan rates and insured by us</li>
<li>Tents, a mess tent and a separate toilet tent</li>
<li>Jeeps between Skardu and Askole, and hotel nights in Skardu</li>
<li>A satellite phone and radios carried throughout the trek</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>What is not included</strong></h2>
<ul>
<li>International flights and the Pakistan visa fee</li>
<li>Travel insurance that covers search and rescue at high altitude, which is compulsory</li>
<li>Tips for the guide, cook and porters</li>
<li>Personal equipment such as boots, a sleeping bag and a down jacket (much of it can be rented in Skardu)</li>
<li>Extra hotel nights if the Skardu flight is cancelled or a road is closed</li>
</ul>
<p>Check your written proposal for the Islamabad to Skardu flight and the Islamabad hotel nights; whether they are included depends on the package you choose.</p>
<h2><strong>Why one quote is higher than another</strong></h2>
<p><strong>Group size matters most.</strong> The guide, the cook and kitchen crew, the jeeps and the camp cost much the same for two trekkers as for ten. Shared between more people, each person pays less, which is why the price for a group of 10 or more is the lowest we offer.</p>
<p><strong>Private dates and custom routes</strong> cost more because the whole team works for your group alone. Extra rest days, single tents, longer stays at Concordia or an add-on such as the <a href="/trekking/gondogoro-la-circuit-trek/">Gondogoro La crossing</a> also change the price.</p>
<p><strong>Be careful with very cheap quotes.</strong> On the Baltoro, the savings usually come from the porters (unpaid rates, no insurance, overloaded), from cutting rest days, or from leaving out the satellite phone. These are exactly the things that matter when something goes wrong.</p>
<h2><strong>How to keep the cost down</strong></h2>
<ul>
<li>Join a scheduled group departure rather than travelling privately.</li>
<li>Travel with friends: a group of 10 or more gets the best price.</li>
<li>Rent bulky gear in Skardu instead of buying it.</li>
<li>Book early, before the summer permits and flights fill up.</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>How payment works</strong></h2>
<p>A deposit of 50% confirms your place. The remaining 50% is paid after you arrive in Islamabad, before you travel to the mountains. Cancellation charges are set out in our <a href="/terms-and-conditions/">terms and conditions</a>.</p>
