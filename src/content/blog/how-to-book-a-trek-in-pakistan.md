<h2><strong>1. Choose the route and the dates</strong></h2>
<p>Start with what you want from the trip and how much time you have. Our <a href="/blog/best-treks-in-pakistan/">best treks in Pakistan</a> guide compares the main routes, and <a href="/blog/best-time-for-trekking-in-pakistan/">the best time for trekking in Pakistan</a> explains the seasons. Most people looking at the Karakoram start with the <a href="/trekking/k2-base-camp-trek/">K2 base camp trek</a> or the <a href="/trekking/gondogoro-la-circuit-trek/">Gondogoro La trek</a>. Scheduled dates are on the <a href="/trip-calendar/">trip calendar</a>.</p>
<h2><strong>2. Ask for a written quote</strong></h2>
<p>Send us your dates, group size and the route through <a href="/plan-a-trip/">Plan a Trip</a> or the <a href="/contact-us/">contact page</a>. We reply with a written itinerary and a quote that lists what is and is not included. The <a href="/blog/k2-base-camp-trek-cost/">K2 base camp trek cost</a> guide explains how prices are built.</p>
<h2><strong>3. Confirm with a deposit</strong></h2>
<p>A 50% deposit confirms your booking. It is due within five days of our confirmation, by bank transfer or cash; we do not take cards. The other 50% is paid after you arrive in Islamabad, before you travel to the mountains. Read our <a href="/terms-and-conditions/">terms and conditions</a> for the cancellation charges before you pay.</p>
<h2><strong>4. Visa, permit and insurance</strong></h2>
<p>Once the booking is confirmed we send the invitation letter you need for the visa. The Baltoro, K2 and the Gondogoro La are a restricted trekking zone, so you apply for a trekking visa, and we apply for the trekking permit. Our <a href="/blog/pakistan-trekking-permits-visa/">trekking permit and visa guide</a> explains each step.</p>
<p>Buy travel insurance that covers your maximum altitude and helicopter rescue, and send us the details. See <a href="/blog/travel-insurance-for-trekking-in-pakistan/">travel insurance for trekking in Pakistan</a>.</p>
<h2><strong>5. Prepare</strong></h2>
<p>Start training early and check your gear against our <a href="/equipment-checklist/">trekking equipment list</a>. For the Baltoro, read <a href="/blog/prepare-for-k2-base-camp-trek/">how to prepare for the K2 base camp trek</a>.</p>
<h2><strong>6. Arrive in Islamabad</strong></h2>
<p>We meet you at the airport, settle the balance, and brief you on the trip. Then it is the flight or the road north to Skardu or Gilgit. If you have a spare day at either end, add an <a href="/tours/islamabad-city-tour/">Islamabad city tour</a> or a <a href="/tours/lahore-city-tour/">Lahore city tour</a>.</p>
