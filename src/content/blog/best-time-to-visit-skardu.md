<h2><strong>The short answer</strong></h2>
<p>For most travellers, <strong>June to September</strong> is the safest window for Skardu. Everything is open: the Deosai road, the high valleys, the jeep tracks to Askole and Hushe, and the trekking routes. If you care more about colour and clear air than about reaching the highest places, <strong>mid-October</strong> is the other great week of the year. Skardu is open all year round, but what you can actually do changes completely between March and November.</p>

<h2><strong>Spring: April and May</strong></h2>
<p>Spring arrives late in Baltistan. Apricot and cherry blossom reach the Skardu valley around the first half of April, usually a week or two after Hunza, and the valley floor turns white and pink while the peaks are still deep in snow. It is the most photogenic time of year at low altitude and the quietest in terms of visitors.</p>
<p>What is not yet possible in spring: Deosai is still under snow and the plateau road normally stays closed until late May or June. The Shigar road toward Askole can be affected by snowmelt and rockfall. High trekking routes are not open.</p>
<p>Spring suits a valley-based trip — lakes, forts, villages and the cold desert. Our <a href="/tours/skardu-shigar-valley-explorer/">Skardu and Shigar valley tour</a> works well in this season, and <a href="/tours/spring-blossom-tour/">the spring blossom tour</a> follows the bloom through Gilgit-Baltistan.</p>

<h2><strong>Summer: June, July and August</strong></h2>
<p>This is the main season, and for good reason. Daytime temperatures in Skardu sit comfortably in the twenties, nights are cool, and almost every road in the region is open.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Deosai</strong> usually opens from late May or June and stays open until around late September. July is when the plateau is covered in wildflowers and when brown bear sightings are most often reported.</li>
<li><strong>Baltoro and K2</strong> trekking runs from late June to early September. July and August are the most settled weeks, and also when the expedition teams are on the glacier.</li>
<li><strong>Hushe, Nangma and the side valleys</strong> are fully accessible, with shepherds up at the summer settlements.</li>
</ul>
<p>Two things to plan around. First, the monsoon does not properly reach Baltistan — it is blocked by the mountains — but it can still bring cloudy spells and road damage on the Karakoram Highway further south, especially in late July and August. Second, this is the busiest season for domestic tourism, so hotels in Skardu fill up and should be booked early.</p>

<h2><strong>Autumn: September and October</strong></h2>
<p>Many people who know the region best will tell you this is the finest time to be in Skardu. The air clears after the summer haze, the poplars and apricot trees turn gold and red, and the mountains stand out sharply. Daytime is pleasant, nights get genuinely cold, and by late October there is frost in the mornings.</p>
<p>Autumn colour normally peaks in Skardu from around <strong>mid-October to the first days of November</strong>, running a week or so behind Hunza. The exact timing shifts each year with the weather, which is why we advise on dates once you tell us which week you can travel. Our <a href="/tours/autumn-hunza-skardu-tour/">autumn tour of Hunza and Skardu</a> is built around this window.</p>
<p>Deosai usually closes to vehicles somewhere between late September and October, depending on the first snowfall, so an autumn trip may or may not include the plateau.</p>

<h2><strong>Winter: November to March</strong></h2>
<p>Winter Skardu is beautiful and demanding. Snow covers the valley, the lakes partly freeze, and the light on the Karakoram is extraordinary. The flight from Islamabad becomes less reliable, the road journey is long and cold, and many hotels either close or operate with limited heating.</p>
<p>Winter travel works for photographers, for travellers who want the valley to themselves, and for those joining the small number of winter expeditions. It does not work for anyone hoping to reach Deosai, Askole or the high valleys.</p>

<h2><strong>Month by month at a glance</strong></h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>March:</strong> cold, quiet, first blossom at the lowest elevations near the end of the month.</li>
<li><strong>April:</strong> blossom season in the Skardu valley; high routes closed.</li>
<li><strong>May:</strong> green valleys, warming days; Deosai usually still closed early in the month.</li>
<li><strong>June:</strong> everything begins to open; Deosai road normally passable; trekking season starts.</li>
<li><strong>July:</strong> peak season; wildflowers on Deosai; settled weather on the Baltoro.</li>
<li><strong>August:</strong> peak season continues; busiest for domestic travel.</li>
<li><strong>September:</strong> clear air, fewer people, excellent trekking until mid-month.</li>
<li><strong>October:</strong> autumn colour; cold nights; Deosai closing.</li>
<li><strong>November to February:</strong> winter; valley travel only, with flight uncertainty.</li>
</ul>

<h2><strong>Getting there, and why it affects your dates</strong></h2>
<p>Skardu is reached by air from Islamabad in under an hour, or by road in roughly eighteen to twenty-four hours along the Karakoram Highway and the Indus valley. The flight is spectacular but weather-dependent and cancellations happen in any season, most often in winter and during cloudy spells.</p>
<p>For that reason we plan every itinerary with a road contingency, and we recommend keeping a spare day at the end of a trip before any international connection. If your dates are tight, the road journey in one direction and the flight in the other is often the most reliable combination.</p>

<h2><strong>So which month should you choose?</strong></h2>
<p>If you want to trek to <a href="/trekking/k2-base-camp-trek/">K2 base camp</a> or cross a high pass, you have a window: late June to early September, and nothing else will do. If you want <a href="/jeep-safaris/deosai-jeep-safari/">Deosai</a>, aim for July or August. If you want colour, photographs and comfortable walking, come in mid-October. If you want blossom and empty roads, come in April.</p>
