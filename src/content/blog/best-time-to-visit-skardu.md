<h2><strong>Skardu by season</strong></h2><p>Skardu changes significantly through the year. Late spring and summer are generally used for road journeys, high-altitude access and major trekking approaches, while autumn is popular for clear light and changing foliage.</p><h2><strong>Match the month to the trip</strong></h2><p>Deosai and remote jeep routes depend on snow clearance and road opening. Expedition and Baltoro trekking windows require separate operational planning. Winter travel is possible for selected valley experiences but should not be presented as the same product.</p><h2><strong>Before booking</strong></h2><p>Confirm flight schedules, road access, hotel availability and the exact route shortly before payment.</p>
