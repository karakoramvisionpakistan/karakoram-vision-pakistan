<p>The trekking season in northern Pakistan is short and depends on height. The high glacier routes of the Karakoram are open for about ten weeks in summer. Lower treks and the valleys open earlier and stay open later. This guide goes through the year and then route by route.</p>
<h2><strong>The year at a glance</strong></h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Late March to mid-April:</strong> blossom season in Hunza and Skardu. Good for tours, too early for high treks.</li>
<li><strong>May:</strong> the jeep road to Fairy Meadows usually opens. The high passes are still under snow.</li>
<li><strong>June:</strong> lower treks are good. The Baltoro is walkable from late June, with more snow and higher rivers.</li>
<li><strong>July and August:</strong> the main season for every high trek, with the most settled weather.</li>
<li><strong>Late August to early September:</strong> quieter camps and clear air, with a growing chance of early snow.</li>
<li><strong>Mid-October to early November:</strong> autumn colour in Hunza and Skardu. The high routes have closed.</li>
<li><strong>Winter:</strong> the valleys are cold and quiet. The trailhead roads close and flights to Skardu are unreliable.</li>
</ul>
<h2><strong>Route by route</strong></h2>
<p><strong><a href="/trekking/k2-base-camp-trek/">K2 Base Camp Trek</a>:</strong> late June to early September. July and August are the most settled, and the expedition teams are on the glacier then. Nights at Concordia fall below freezing in every month of the season.</p>
<p><strong><a href="/trekking/gondogoro-la-circuit-trek/">Gondogoro La</a>:</strong> mid-July to late August. June is usually too early, because the pass still carries deep snow and the ropes may not be fixed. Late August is quieter, but the pass can close with little warning.</p>
<p><strong><a href="/trekking/biafo-hispar-trek/">Biafo Hispar Snow Lake</a>:</strong> July and August.</p>
<p><strong><a href="/trekking/fairy-meadows-base-camp-trek/">Nanga Parbat base camp via Fairy Meadows</a>:</strong> the road is open from about May to October. June to September gives the clearest views, and mornings are usually clearer than afternoons.</p>
<h2><strong>Temperatures</strong></h2>
<p>Summer days on the lower Baltoro reach the mid-twenties Celsius in full sun. At Concordia, at 4,600 metres, nights are below freezing all season, so a sleeping bag rated to minus ten Celsius or colder is needed. Fairy Meadows, at about 3,300 metres, is cold at night even in July.</p>
<h2><strong>Flights and spare days</strong></h2>
<p>The flight from Islamabad to Skardu is flown by sight and is cancelled whenever cloud closes in, in every season. When it is, we drive the Karakoram Highway instead, which takes two days. Every itinerary carries a spare day for this, and we advise leaving a day before your international flight home.</p>
