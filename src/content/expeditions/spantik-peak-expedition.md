<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2><p>Spantik, 7,027m, is the mountain most climbers in Pakistan name when asked for a realistic first 7,000m peak. From the Nagar side the normal route is a long snow climb without sustained technical difficulty; from the Arandu side rises the Golden Pillar, one of the hardest lines in the Karakoram. The same mountain carries both names - Spantik in Baltistan, Golden Peak in Nagar, after the colour the face takes at sunset.</p><p><strong>What this programme is.</strong> Thirty-five days from Islamabad: the approach from Nagar or Arandu, acclimatisation rotations through the camps, a summit window and the return.</p><p><strong>Who it is for.</strong> Climbers comfortable with crampons, ice axe and roped glacier travel who want their first serious altitude. Previous experience above 5,000m is strongly recommended.</p><p><strong>What we provide.</strong> Permits and liaison officer, transport, hotels, porters for the approach, base camp with cook and kitchen, and guides who know both approaches to the mountain.</p><p>A supported expedition plan for Spantik Peak with permit coordination, approach trekking, base-camp logistics and weather contingency.
