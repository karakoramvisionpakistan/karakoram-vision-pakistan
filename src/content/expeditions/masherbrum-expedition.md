<h2><strong>About this expedition</strong></h2>
<p>Masherbrum (7,821 m) rises at the head of the Masherbrum valley above Hushe, the last village in the valley. It was the first peak labelled by the 1856 survey of the Karakoram, as K1; K2 was the second. The standard route is from the south-east, approached from Hushe in two days.</p>
<h2><strong>Base camp service or full board</strong></h2>
<p><strong>Base camp service</strong> covers the permit, liaison officer, transport, hotels, porters to base camp and a full base camp. <strong>Full board</strong> adds the support above base camp that your team chooses, such as high-altitude porters, oxygen and climbing gear, each quoted separately.</p>
<h2><strong>Permit and royalty</strong></h2>
<p>Masherbrum is in the 7,000-metre band, with a royalty of USD 2,000. See our <a href="/blog/pakistan-mountaineering-royalty-fees/">royalty fee guide</a>.</p>
<h2><strong>Who it is for</strong></h2>
<p>Experienced alpinists with previous high-altitude climbs. Hushe is also the base for <a href="/expeditions/laila-peak-expedition/">Laila Peak</a> and the end point of the <a href="/trekking/gondogoro-la-circuit-trek/">Gondogoro La trek</a>.</p>
