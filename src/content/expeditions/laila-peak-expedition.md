<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2>
<p>Laila Peak is one of the most photographed mountains in Pakistan and one of the most recognisable shapes in the Karakoram - a thin, leaning spire above the Gondogoro glacier in the Hushe valley. Its north-west face has been both climbed and skied, and it remains a serious alpine objective rather than a trekking peak.</p>
<p><strong>What this programme is.</strong> Thirty days from Islamabad: road travel to Skardu and the Hushe valley, a few days of approach trekking up to the Gondogoro glacier that doubles as acclimatisation, the climbing period, and the return.</p>
<p><strong>Who it is for.</strong> Alpinists with solid snow and ice experience. The face is sustained and steep, and teams are expected to be self-sufficient on the route.</p>
<p><strong>What we provide.</strong> Permits and liaison officer where required, transport from Islamabad and Skardu, porters from Hushe, base camp with cook and kitchen, and the local knowledge of a team that works in this valley every season. Laila Peak combines naturally with a Gondogoro La crossing or with time in Nangma valley.</p>
