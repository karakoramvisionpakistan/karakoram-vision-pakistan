<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2>
<p>Broad Peak, 8,051m, stands beside K2 at the head of the Baltoro and shares its approach, its base camp area and its weather. The route is less technical than its neighbour, which is why many climbers choose it as a first 8,000m peak - but the summit ridge is long, exposed and higher than it looks from below, and the final hour to the true summit defeats many attempts.</p>
<p><strong>What this programme is.</strong> Fifty days from Islamabad, covering the Baltoro trek in, acclimatisation rotations, a realistic summit window and the return. Spare time is built in because the Baltoro rarely runs to schedule.</p>
<p><strong>Who it is for.</strong> Climbers with solid high-altitude experience and comfort on snow and mixed ground at extreme altitude.</p>
<p><strong>What we provide.</strong> Climbing and park permits, liaison officer expenses, the mountaineering visa invitation letter, airport transfers, hotels, meals through the trek and at base camp, and all logistics up to base camp. Teams often combine Broad Peak with K2 or a Gasherbrum peak in the same season, which we can plan as a single permit and logistics package.</p>
