It is the ninth highest mountain on earth and carries a reputation earned in the early attempts of the last century - the Rupal face above its southern side is the highest mountain wall in the world.</p><p><strong>What this programme is.</strong> Forty-two days from Islamabad, covering the approach, acclimatisation rotations, a summit window and weather contingency. Unlike the Baltoro peaks, Nanga Parbat is reached from the Karakoram Highway rather than from Skardu, which shortens the approach considerably.</p><p><strong>Who it is for.</strong> Experienced high-altitude climbers. Most teams use the Diamir side; tell us your intended route and we will plan permits, porters and base camp around it.</p><p><strong>What we provide.</strong> Climbing permit and liaison officer, the invitation letter for the mountaineering visa, transport along the Karakoram Highway, hotels, porters for the approach, and base camp with kitchen and dining.
