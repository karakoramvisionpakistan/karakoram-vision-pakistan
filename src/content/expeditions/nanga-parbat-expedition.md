<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2><p>Nanga Parbat, 8,126m, stands alone at the western end of the Himalaya, visible for a hundred kilometres along the Karakoram Highway. It is the ninth highest mountain on earth and carries a reputation earned in the early attempts of the last century - the Rupal face above its southern side is the highest mountain wall in the world.</p><p><strong>What this programme is.</strong> Forty-two days from Islamabad, covering the approach, acclimatisation rotations, a summit window and weather contingency. Unlike the Baltoro peaks, Nanga Parbat is reached from the Karakoram Highway rather than from Skardu, which shortens the approach considerably.</p><p><strong>Who it is for.</strong> Experienced high-altitude climbers. Most teams use the Diamir side; tell us your intended route and we will plan permits, porters and base camp around it.</p><p><strong>What we provide.</strong> Climbing permit and liaison officer, the invitation letter for the mountaineering visa, transport along the Karakoram Highway, hotels, porters for the approach, and base camp with kitchen and dining. High-altitude support and oxygen are arranged on request.</p><p><strong>Fact &amp; Figures:</strong><br>Duration of Expedition:&nbsp; &nbsp;42 Days Islamabad to Islamabad<br>Hotel Nights: 05 Nights&nbsp; Camping Nights:&nbsp; &nbsp;03 Nights<br>Days for Climbing: 34 Days&nbsp; &nbsp; Group Size:&nbsp; 08+&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;</p><p>&nbsp; Best Time: 15 May- 1 July<br>Type of Expedition: Unguided (services up to base camp only)</p><p><strong>PROGRAM</strong></p>
