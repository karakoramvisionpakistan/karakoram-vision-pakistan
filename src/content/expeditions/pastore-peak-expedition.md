<h2><strong>About this expedition</strong></h2>
<p>Pastore Peak (6,209 m) stands near Concordia, among the great peaks of the Baltoro. It is one of the few summits in this part of the Karakoram that a fit trekker with some crampon experience can realistically climb, and the view from the top takes in K2, Broad Peak and the Gasherbrums.</p>
<p>The programme follows our <a href="/trekking/k2-base-camp-trek/">K2 Base Camp Trek</a> to Concordia, visits K2 base camp for acclimatization, and then moves to Pastore base camp for the climb.</p>
<h2><strong>Permit</strong></h2>
<p>Pastore Peak is below 6,500 metres, so it is climbed on a trekking permit. The Baltoro is a restricted trekking zone, so you climb with a licensed guide. See our <a href="/blog/pakistan-mountaineering-royalty-fees/">royalty fee guide</a>.</p>
<h2><strong>Who it is for</strong></h2>
<p>Strong trekkers who want a first Karakoram summit. You should be comfortable with long glacier days and have used crampons and an ice axe before.</p>
