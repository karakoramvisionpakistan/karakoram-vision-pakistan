<h2><strong>About this expedition</strong></h2>
<p>Rakaposhi (7,788 m) stands directly above the Nagar and Hunza valleys and is one of the most recognisable mountains in the Karakoram. Its base camp is reached in two or three days from Minapin, just off the Karakoram Highway, so the approach is short compared with the Baltoro peaks. The climb itself is long, steep and committing.</p>
<p>The first ascent was made in 1958 by a British-Pakistani expedition. The mountain has seen relatively few ascents since.</p>
<h2><strong>Base camp service or full board</strong></h2>
<p>We offer two levels of service. <strong>Base camp service</strong> covers the permit, liaison officer, transport, hotels, porters to base camp and a full base camp. <strong>Full board</strong> adds the support above base camp that your team chooses, such as high-altitude porters, oxygen and climbing gear, each quoted separately.</p>
<h2><strong>Permit and royalty</strong></h2>
<p>Rakaposhi is in the 7,000-metre band, with a royalty of USD 2,000. See our <a href="/blog/pakistan-mountaineering-royalty-fees/">royalty fee guide</a> for the other peaks.</p>
<h2><strong>Who it is for</strong></h2>
<p>Experienced alpinists with previous high-altitude climbs. This is not a first expedition peak. If you want a 7,000-metre peak to start with, look at our <a href="/expeditions/spantik-peak-expedition/">Spantik expedition</a>. Trekkers can walk to the same base camp on our <a href="/trekking/rakaposhi-base-camp-trek/">Rakaposhi base camp trek</a>.</p>
