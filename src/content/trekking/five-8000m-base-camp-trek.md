<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2><p>Pakistan has five mountains over 8,000 metres, and this trek visits the base camp of every one. Four of them stand together at the head of the Baltoro Glacier: K2 (8,611 m), Broad Peak (8,051 m), Gasherbrum I (8,080 m) and Gasherbrum II (8,035 m). The fifth, Nanga Parbat (8,126 m), stands alone at the western end of the Himalaya and is reached from Fairy Meadows.</p><p>The programme is our <a href="/trekking/k2-base-camp-trek/">K2 Base Camp Trek</a> with two additions: three days from Concordia to the Gasherbrum base camp and back, and four days at Fairy Meadows on the way home. It is the longest trek we run and the most complete way to see the high mountains of Pakistan in one visit.</p><h2><strong>The five base camps</strong></h2><ul><li><p><strong>Broad Peak</strong>: passed on the Godwin-Austen Glacier on the way to K2.</p></li><li><p><strong>K2</strong>: the strip of moraine below the south face, reached the same day.</p></li><li><p><strong>Gasherbrum I and Gasherbrum II</strong>: one shared base camp on the Abruzzi Glacier, two days beyond Concordia.</p></li><li><p><strong>Nanga Parbat</strong>: the Raikot side, a day walk from Fairy Meadows.</p></li></ul><h2><strong>How hard is it?</strong></h2><p>This is a strenuous trek. It is not technical, but it means about seventeen days on glacier moraine, ten nights above 4,000 metres and a highest camp at about 5,150 metres. You should have done at least one long high-altitude trek before. The itinerary includes an acclimatization day at Paiju and a recovery day at Concordia, and we keep both.</p><h2><strong>When to go</strong></h2><p>Late June to mid-August. The Gasherbrum base camp is occupied by expeditions in this period and the upper Baltoro is easier to cross. The Fairy Meadows section is open from about May to October, so the Karakoram decides the dates.</p><h2><strong>Permits and paperwork</strong></h2><p>The Baltoro section is a restricted trekking zone inside Central Karakoram National Park and needs a trekking permit and a licensed guide. The Fairy Meadows section has simpler formalities. We arrange all of it. See our <a href="/blog/pakistan-trekking-permits-visa/">permit and visa guide</a> for the steps.</p><h2><strong>Planning notes</strong></h2><p>This is a standard programme and a starting point. Dates, group size, hotel category and the final price are confirmed in a written proposal. If you have less time, the same route without Fairy Meadows visits four base camps and ends with the flight from Skardu. <a href="/plan-a-trip/">Send us your dates</a> for a proposal.</p>
