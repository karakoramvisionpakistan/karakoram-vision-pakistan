<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2>
<p>Shimshal is the highest village in upper Hunza and the home of Wakhi herders who take their animals over the Shimshal Pass to summer pastures every year. The trek follows their route through the Shimshal Pamir to the pass at 4,735 metres, with its lakes and wide grassland on the watershed between the Indus and the Tarim basins.</p>
<h2><strong>How hard is it?</strong></h2>
<p>A long, high trek on rough paths, with several days above 4,000 metres. No technical climbing. Previous multi-day trekking is recommended.</p>
<h2><strong>When to go</strong></h2>
<p>June to September. The herders move to the pastures beyond the pass in early summer and return in the autumn.</p>
<h2><strong>Getting there</strong></h2>
<p>Shimshal is reached by jeep from Karimabad in four to five hours. See our <a href="/destinations/hunza/">Hunza travel guide</a>.</p>
