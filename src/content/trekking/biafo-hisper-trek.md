<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2><p>Few treks anywhere cross an entire mountain range on ice. The Biafo Hispar traverse links Baltistan with Hunza along 116 kilometres of connected glacier - the longest glacial system outside the polar regions - crossing the Hispar La at 5,151m and passing Snow Lake, the great white basin that captured the imagination of early Karakoram explorers.</p><p><strong>Who it suits.</strong> Experienced trekkers who are comfortable with long days, roped glacier travel and three weeks away from roads, shops and mobile signal. There is no technical climbing, but the commitment is real: once the team is on the Biafo there is no quick way out.</p><p><strong>When to go.</strong> Early July to the end of August, when snow bridges and crevasse conditions on Snow Lake are at their most reliable.</p><p><strong>What we handle.</strong> Permits, transport to Askole, porters for both sides of the traverse, camp equipment, cook, and guides who have crossed the pass before. The route finishes in Hispar village in Nagar, from where the journey continues by jeep to Karimabad and the Karakoram Highway.</p><p><strong>Fact &amp; Figures:</strong></p><p><strong>Altitude: &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; 5151M &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; Range: &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;Karakoram &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; Location: &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; Biafo Glacier&nbsp; Duration: &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; 22 Days &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>Hotel Nights: &nbsp; &nbsp;10 &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; Camping Nights: &nbsp; &nbsp;12 &nbsp;&nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>Type of Trek: &nbsp; &nbsp; Strenuous &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;Zone type: &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;Open &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;</strong></p><p><strong>Ideal Time: &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;1st July - End of August</strong></p><p>An interesting yet less frequented traverse from Baltistan to Hunza valley crossing over the Biafo-Hisper pass at an altitude of 5151m. The combined length of 116 kms Biafo and Hisper glaciers forms the longest glacier after the poles. It offers excellent view of group of mountain and glaciers. Above all the Snow Lake has the charms of none. During this trek you will find a stunning experience of sunrise and set over the Karakoram mountains. The unique bio-diversity with natural flora and fauna gives great chances to see wildlife especially the Himalayan brown bears. The trek involves crossing of high pass, snow lands and many glaciers and streams. This trek requires good fitness and courage for a lasting nature reward. Driving through the Shiger valley up to Askoli village, the trek begins and enters into a corridor full of savage beauty. Traversing through the Biafo Glacier and snow lake the trek ascends up to the pass. After crossing the Pass and walking on Hisper glacier we will reach to the Hisper village in the next five days.
