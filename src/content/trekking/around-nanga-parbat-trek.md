<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2>
<p>This trek crosses Nanga Parbat (8,126 m) from south to north. It starts in the Rupal valley under the Rupal face, one of the highest mountain walls on earth, follows the valley west, crosses the Mazeno La at about 5,400 metres, and works around the western side of the mountain to Fairy Meadows. Despite the name, it goes about halfway round the mountain.</p>
<h2><strong>How hard is it?</strong></h2>
<p>Strenuous. The Mazeno La is a glacier pass crossed with ropes, harness and crampons, and several days are spent high and far from roads. You need previous high-altitude trekking and a steady head on glacier ground.</p>
<h2><strong>Fairy Meadows at the end</strong></h2>
<p>The trek ends at Fairy Meadows, at about 3,300 metres, with a day walk to Nanga Parbat base camp. If you want only this part, see our <a href="/trekking/fairy-meadows-base-camp-trek/">Nanga Parbat base camp trek</a> and the <a href="/destinations/fairy-meadows/">Fairy Meadows travel guide</a>.</p>
