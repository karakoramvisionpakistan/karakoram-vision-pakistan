<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2><p>The K2 Concordia trek follows the Baltoro Glacier from the village of Askole to Concordia, the open junction of ice where the Baltoro and Godwin-Austen glaciers meet. Four of the world's fourteen 8,000-metre peaks stand around the head of this glacier: K2, Broad Peak, Gasherbrum I and Gasherbrum II. This is the same approach used by the climbing expeditions, walked at a trekker's pace with a full camp crew.</p><p>This itinerary turns around at Concordia. It suits trekkers who want the Baltoro and the view of K2 without the extra-long day up the Godwin-Austen Glacier to the foot of the mountain. If you want to stand at base camp itself, look at our <a href="/trekking/k2-base-camp-trek/">K2 Base Camp Trek</a>, which adds that day.</p><h2><strong>Concordia: what you see</strong></h2><p>From camp, K2 (8,611 m) fills the head of the Godwin-Austen Glacier to the north. Broad Peak (8,051 m) is the long three-summited wall to its right. Gasherbrum IV closes the Baltoro to the east, with Mitre Peak directly above the tents. The best light is in the first and last hour of the day, which is why the programme gives Concordia two nights.</p><h2><strong>How hard is it?</strong></h2><p>There is no technical climbing, but this is a demanding trek. You walk five to eight hours a day for about thirteen days, most of it on the loose rock that covers the glacier, and you sleep above 4,000 metres for six nights. Fit hill walkers who have done multi-day treks before manage it well. The acclimatization day at Paiju is built in and we do not shorten it.</p><h2><strong>When to go</strong></h2><p>The season runs from late June to early September. July and August have the most settled weather and are also when the expeditions are on the glacier. See our guide to <a href="/blog/prepare-for-k2-base-camp-trek/">preparing for the Baltoro</a> for training and kit.</p><h2><strong>Permits and paperwork</strong></h2><p>The route lies inside Central Karakoram National Park and is a restricted trekking zone, so foreign trekkers need a trekking permit and must travel with a licensed guide. We handle the permit, park fees and registration in Skardu. Our <a href="/blog/pakistan-trekking-permits-visa/">permit and visa guide</a> explains the steps and timing.</p><h2><strong>Planning notes</strong></h2><p>This is a standard programme and a starting point. Dates, group size, hotel category and the final price are confirmed in a written proposal. Flights between Islamabad and Skardu depend on the weather, and the last day of the programme is there to absorb a delay. <a href="/plan-a-trip/">Send us your dates</a> for a proposal.</p>
