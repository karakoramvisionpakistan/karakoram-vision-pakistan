<p><strong><em>Trekking to Nangma Valley (The Yosemite of Pakistan)</em></strong></p><p><strong>Where is Nangma Valley?</strong></p><p>Nangma Valley is located in the Ghanche district of Gilgit-Baltistan, about a 5-hour drive from the bustling trekker’s hub of Skardu. It is part of the Central Karakoram National Park, and one of the more accessible treks to do in the region. The gateway to Nangma Valley is the village of Kanday, or Kanday, or one of the many other spelling variations you’ll find in the area.</p><p><strong>How To Get To Nangma Valley.</strong></p><p>Nangma Valley is no easy feat to get to, and that’s not even including the hiking. Your first step would be to make it to Skardu, the largest city in the Baltistan region. It has a small but bustling airport with multiple daily flights from both Islamabad and Lahore. The flight is about 45 minutes long, and likely the most scenic you will ever take in your life. Flights are very sensitive to weather and can be prone to cancellation. <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://www.airsial.com/"><strong>AirSial</strong></a> has been the most reliable airline for Skardu, followed by <strong>Pakistan International Airlines</strong>. <strong>AirBlue</strong> is a last resort. Alternatively, <strong>buses</strong> from Islamabad go to Skardu and even all the way to Khaplu and then by <strong>jeep or car</strong> to Kanday village and from there the trek to Nangma Valley will start.</p><p><strong>Organizing the Nangma Valley Trek.</strong></p><p>Booking a Nangma Valley tour is easy. It just depends on how much you want to pay. The most affordable way to do the Nangma Valley trek is to make your own way to Kanday village and arrange for guides and porters there.</p><p><strong>PROGRAM</strong></p><p><strong>Day 1: Arrive in Islamabad and transfer to hotel </strong></p><p><strong>Day 2: Fly to Skardu</strong></p><p><strong>Day 3: Drive to Kanday</strong></p><p><strong>Day 4: Trek to Minglu Broq</strong></p><p><strong>Day 5: Trek to Nangma Vallet Base Camp</strong></p><p><strong>Day 6: Photography day</strong></p><p><strong>Day 7: Trek back to Kanday</strong></p><p><strong>Day 8: Drive back to Skardu</strong></p><p><strong>Day 9: Fly to Islamabad </strong></p><p><strong>Day 10: Fly out </strong></p>
