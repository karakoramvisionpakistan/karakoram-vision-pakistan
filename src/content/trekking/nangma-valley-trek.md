<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2>
<p>Nangma is the valley climbers call the Yosemite of Pakistan. Above Kanday village in the Hushe region, soft green pasture runs between vertical granite walls and towers that draw big-wall climbers from around the world. For trekkers the attraction is the same scenery without the ropes: an easy walk in, a base camp on the valley floor, and days spent wandering among the boulders and side valleys beneath the walls.</p>
<p><strong>Who it suits.</strong> Beginners, families and anyone short of time. The walking days are short, the base camp is reached in a day or two from the village, and the altitude is gentle compared with the Baltoro routes.</p>
<p><strong>When to go.</strong> June to September, when the shepherds are at the summer settlements and the valley is green.</p>
<p><strong>What we handle.</strong> Jeep transport from Skardu through the Shyok valley and the Hushe road - around five hours - porters from Kanday village, camp equipment, cook and guide. For climbing teams we can arrange extended camp support for stays of several weeks.</p>

It is part of the Central Karakoram National Park, and one of the more accessible treks to do in the region. The gateway to Nangma Valley is the village of Kanday, or Kanday, or one of the many other spelling variations you’ll find in the area.</p><p><strong>How To Get To Nangma Valley.</strong></p><p>Nangma Valley is no easy feat to get to, and that’s not even including the hiking. Your first step would be to make it to Skardu, the largest city in the Baltistan region. It has a small but bustling airport with multiple daily flights from both Islamabad and Lahore. The flight is about 45 minutes long, and likely the most scenic you will ever take in your life. Flights are very sensitive to weather and can be prone to cancellation. <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="https://www.airsial.com/"><strong>AirSial</strong></a> has been the most reliable airline for Skardu, followed by <strong>Pakistan International Airlines</strong>. <strong>AirBlue</strong> is a last resort. Alternatively, <strong>buses</strong> from Islamabad go to Skardu and even all the way to Khaplu and then by <strong>jeep or car</strong> to Kanday village and from there the trek to Nangma Valley will start.</p><p><strong>Organizing the Nangma Valley Trek.</strong></p><p>Booking a Nangma Valley tour is easy.
