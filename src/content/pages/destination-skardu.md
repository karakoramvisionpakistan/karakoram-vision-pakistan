<p><strong>Overview</strong></p><p>Skardu is a high-altitude mountain city in Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan, serving as the main gateway to several of the world's highest peaks, including K2.</p><p><strong>Geography and Location</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Elevation:</strong> Sits at an average height of about 2,200 to 2,500 meters (7,200 to 8,200 feet) above sea level.&nbsp;</p></li></ul><ul><li><p><strong>Rivers:</strong> Located at the meeting point of the Indus and Shigar rivers.&nbsp;</p></li><li><p><strong>Surroundings:</strong> Framed by the massive peaks of the Karakoram range, including Gasherbrum and Broad Peak.&nbsp;</p></li></ul><p><strong>Top Attractions</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Deosai National Park:</strong> A vast, high-altitude plateau known for wildflowers and the Himalayan brown bear.</p></li></ul><ul><li><p><strong>Cold Deserts:</strong> Features unique high-altitude sand dunes at Katpana and Sarfaranga.&nbsp;</p></li></ul><ul><li><p><strong>Lakes:</strong> Scenic turquoise waters sparkle at Shangrila Lake, Upper Kachura Lake, and Sadpara Lake.&nbsp;</p></li></ul><ul><li><p><strong>Historical Sites:</strong> Ancient architecture stands strong at the 16th-century Kharpocho Fort and the Manthal Buddha Rock.&nbsp;</p></li></ul><p><strong>Travel and Best Time to Visit</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Peak Season:</strong> The ideal time to visit for clear weather and accessible roads is from <strong>May through July</strong>.&nbsp;</p></li><li><p><strong>Getting There:</strong> Accessible via Skardu Airport with regular domestic flights from Islamabad, Lahore and Karachi in summer time or by road via Karakoram Highway. Flights are often delayed or canceled due to weather conditions.&nbsp;</p></li></ul><h2><strong>Gateway to the eastern Karakoram</strong></h2><p>Skardu connects travelers with lakes, heritage valleys, cold deserts, the Deosai Plateau and major trekking approaches.</p><h2><strong>Planning considerations</strong></h2><p>Flights are weather-sensitive and road journeys require realistic travel time. Remote valleys and high plateaus have seasonal access.</p>
