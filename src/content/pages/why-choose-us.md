<p>We are a Skardu company. The people who plan your trip are the people who run it on the ground, with no agent in between, and most of our team grew up in the valleys you will travel through.</p>
<h2>Local people, licensed guides</h2>
<p>Our guides hold government licences, most speak fluent English, and the majority are certified by the Alpine Club of Pakistan. They come from these valleys rather than being brought in for the season, which matters when the weather turns or a route has changed since last year. Meet them on our <a href="/our-team/">team page</a>.</p>
<h2>Porters and staff treated properly</h2>
<p>Every member of staff and every porter is insured by us before a trek or expedition begins. Porters are paid at the official Gilgit-Baltistan rates, and loads are kept to the agreed limits.</p>
<h2>Safety you can see</h2>
<ul>
<li>A satellite phone and radios travel on every trek, not only on expeditions.</li>
<li>Guides carry a first-aid kit and oxygen and watch the group every day.</li>
<li>Itineraries gain height slowly, with rest days where the route needs them.</li>
<li>Foreign trekkers are registered in Skardu before departure, and our staff stay with the group throughout.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Your dates, your route</h2>
<p>Most trips run privately, on the dates you choose and at the pace that suits your group. Tell us what you want from the mountains and we will shape the itinerary around it, from a week in Hunza to an 8,000 m expedition.</p>
<h2>Before you book</h2>
<p>Choose a trip that matches your fitness and experience; if you are unsure, ask us and we will tell you honestly. Make sure the names you give us match your passports, and that your insurance covers the activities you will do. The full conditions are in our <a href="/terms-and-conditions/">terms and conditions</a>, and the booking steps are in our <a href="/faqs/">FAQs</a>.</p>
