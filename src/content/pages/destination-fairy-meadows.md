<h2><strong>Overview</strong></h2><p>Fairy Meadows is a lush alpine grassland situated at an altitude of 3,300 meters (10,800 feet) in the Raikot Valley, Diamer District, Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan, famous for providing a dramatic front-row view of the majestic Nanga Parbat (8,126 m), the world's ninth-highest peak.</p><ul><li><p><strong>Local Name:</strong> Known locally as <em>Joot</em> (or <em>Jaguth</em>).</p></li><li><p><strong>Origin of Name:</strong> Named <em>Märchenwiese</em> ("fairy tale meadows") by German mountaineers and later termed "Fairy Meadows" by Austrian climber Hermann Buhl during the 1953 Nanga Parbat expedition.&nbsp;</p></li><li><p><strong>Status:</strong> Declared a National Park by the Government of Pakistan in 1995.&nbsp;</p></li></ul><p><strong>How to Get There</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Access Point:</strong> Turn off the Karakoram Highway at Raikot Bridge.</p></li><li><p><strong>Jeep Track:</strong> A 15-kilometer narrow, unpaved mountain jeep track runs from Raikot Bridge to Tattu (Tato) village (taking 1.5 to 2.5 hours). This track is strictly restricted to local 4x4 jeeps driven by experienced community drivers. Round-trip jeep fares cost approximately PKR 20,000 to 25000 per vehicle (which fits up to 4 passengers).&nbsp;</p></li><li><p><strong>Trekking Portion:</strong> From Tattu village, a 5-kilometer hiking trail leads up to Fairy Meadows, taking about 3 to 4 hours on foot. Porters and horses are available in Tattu village for hire (horses cost around PKR 6000).</p></li></ul><p><strong>Best Time to Visit</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Tourist Season:</strong> Runs from April through September.</p></li><li><p><strong>Peak Window:</strong> June to August offers the clearest weather, blooming green plateaus, and optimal visibility of Nanga Parbat, though summer nights remain chilly. The area closes and receives heavy snow during winter.</p></li></ul><p><strong>Activities and Accommodations</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Hiking &amp; Trekking:</strong> Serves as the primary base camp launchpad for hikes toward Beyal Camp and the Nanga Parbat Base Camp.</p></li><li><p><strong>Stays:</strong> Visitors can lodge in rustic wooden alpine cabins, cottages, or designated campsites.&nbsp;</p></li></ul><p></p><p>If you are planning a trip, let us know:</p><ul><li><p>Your <strong>starting location</strong> or travel dates</p></li><li><p>If you need an itinerary combining Fairy Meadows with <strong>Hunza, Skardu</strong> or other regions</p></li></ul><p>We can help you plan your travel logistics.</p>
