<h2><strong>The road to the great mountains</strong></h2><p>Shigar lies about thirty kilometres northeast of Skardu, forty-five minutes by road, and the valley runs some hundred and seventy kilometres from there up to Askole — the last village before the Baltoro. Every K2 expedition, every Baltoro trek and every Gondogoro La crossing passes through this valley. For travellers who are not going that far, Shigar offers a fort-palace, orchards, cold desert and one of the most pleasant short drives in Baltistan.</p><h2><strong>Getting there</strong></h2><p>By road from Skardu, crossing the Indus and following the Shigar river north — a short and scenic drive suitable for any vehicle as far as Shigar town. Beyond that, toward Askole, the road deteriorates steadily and becomes 4x4 only, with river crossings and rockfall sections. The full drive to Askole takes six to eight hours from Skardu depending on conditions.</p><h2><strong>What to see</strong></h2><p><strong>Shigar Fort</strong>, known locally as Fong Khar, the palace on the rock, is a four-hundred-year-old raja's residence restored by the Aga Khan Trust for Culture and now operating as a heritage hotel and museum. The woodwork and the setting are both exceptional.</p><p><strong>The valley floor</strong> is fertile and green with apricot, apple and walnut, fed by the Shigar river and irrigation channels — a striking contrast to the bare rock above it and the sand of the cold desert nearby.</p><p><strong>The Sarfaranga cold desert</strong> lies between Skardu and Shigar, a wide expanse of dunes between the mountains and one of the highest deserts in the world.</p><p><strong>Askole</strong>, at the top of the valley, is the roadhead for the Karakoram — a small village where trek teams meet their porters and the road ends.</p><h2><strong>How long to stay</strong></h2><p>Half a day covers the fort and the drive; one night at Shigar Fort makes it a destination in itself. Our <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/tours/skardu-shigar-valley-explorer/">Skardu and Shigar valley tour</a> includes the valley over seven days from Skardu, and the <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/jeep-safaris/shigar-basho-khaplu-jeep-safari/">Shigar and Khaplu jeep safari</a> continues into the Basho meadows and on to Khaplu.</p><h2><strong>When to go</strong></h2><p>April and May bring blossom to the orchards; June to September is the main season and the only window for travel beyond Shigar toward Askole; late September and October bring autumn colour. The upper valley road is affected by snowmelt and rockfall in spring and by snow in winter.</p><h2><strong>Where to stay</strong></h2><p>Shigar Fort is the standout, with heritage rooms in the restored palace and a garden. The town also has simpler guest houses, and most travellers otherwise base themselves in Skardu, forty-five minutes away.</p><h2><strong>Practical notes</strong></h2><p>Coverage is adequate in Shigar town and disappears up the valley. Nothing beyond Shigar takes cards, and there is no fuel after the town. Anyone travelling toward Askole should be prepared for a long, rough day and for the road to be closed by rockfall or river at short notice — which is exactly why trek itineraries build in spare days.</p><h2><strong>Journeys through Shigar</strong></h2><p>The <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/trekking/k2-base-camp-trek/">K2 base camp trek</a> and the <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/trekking/gondogoro-la-circuit-trek/">Gondogoro La circuit</a> both begin with the drive up this valley to Askole, as do the Baltoro expeditions. The <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/trekking/thalle-la-trek/">Thalle La trek</a> starts from the Shigar side and crosses to Khaplu.</p><h2><strong>Related journeys</strong></h2><ul><li><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/tours/skardu-shigar-valley-explorer/">Skardu and Shigar valley tour</a></p></li><li><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/trekking/k2-base-camp-trek/">K2 base camp trek</a></p></li><li><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/jeep-safaris/shigar-basho-khaplu-jeep-safari/">Shigar and Khaplu jeep safari</a></p></li></ul><p></p>
