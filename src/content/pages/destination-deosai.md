<h2><strong>Overview</strong></h2><p>Deosai National Park is the world's second-highest alpine plateau, sitting at an average elevation of 4,114 meters (13,497 feet) in Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan.&nbsp;</p><ul><li><p><strong>Location:</strong> Spans between Skardu District and Astore District</p></li><li><p><strong>Meaning:</strong> Known locally as the "Land of Giants" (<em>Deo</em> meaning giant, <em>Sai</em> meaning shadow/place) and <em>Ghbiarsa</em> in Balti, meaning "summer's place".</p></li><li><p><strong>Landscape:</strong> Vast, rolling treeless plains covered with wildflowers and crisscrossed by crystal-clear streams during the summer.</p></li></ul><p><strong>Wildlife &amp; Nature</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Himalayan Brown Bear:</strong> Established in 1993, the park serves as a protected natural habitat and vital sanctuary for the critically endangered Himalayan brown bear.</p></li><li><p><strong>Other Fauna:</strong> Home to golden marmots, Tibetan wolves, snow leopards, and numerous migratory birds.</p></li><li><p><strong>Sheosar Lake:</strong> A pristine, deep-blue alpine lake located inside the park at over 4,100 meters elevation.</p></li></ul><ul><li><p><strong>Best Time to Visit:</strong> July and August offer the best weather (5°C to 20°C) and peak wildflower blooms.</p></li><li><p><strong>Accessibility:</strong> Heavy snow closes the plateau from November through May it is typically accessible only from June to mid-October via 4x4 jeeps from Skardu or Astore.</p></li></ul><p></p><p><strong>If you are planning a trip, let us know:</strong></p><ul><li><p>Are you departing from <strong>Islamabad, Gilgit,</strong> <strong>Skardu or Astore</strong>?</p></li><li><p>Do you plan on <strong>day-tripping or overnight camping</strong>?</p></li></ul><p></p>
