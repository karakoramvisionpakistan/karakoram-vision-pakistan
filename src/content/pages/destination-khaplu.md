<h2><strong>Baltistan's eastern valley</strong></h2><p>Khaplu sits at around 2,600m on the Shyok river, two and a half to three hours east of Skardu, and is the administrative centre of Ghanche district. It was historically the second-largest kingdom in Baltistan, ruled for centuries by the Yabgo dynasty, and that history is still standing: a palace above the town, old mosques in the villages, and irrigation channels that have watered the same apricot orchards for generations.</p><p>It is greener and gentler than Skardu, and quieter than Hunza. For many travellers it is the valley they wish they had given another day to.</p><h2><strong>Getting there</strong></h2><p>By road from Skardu, following the Indus and then the Shyok river east — two and a half to three hours on a reasonable road, with the valley opening up as you go. The same road continues to Hushe, the last village before the Masherbrum and Gondogoro peaks.</p><h2><strong>What to see</strong></h2><p><strong>Khaplu Palace</strong>, built in the 1840s and restored by the Aga Khan Trust for Culture, stands above the town looking down the valley. It operates as a hotel and museum, and the restoration is widely regarded as one of the best heritage projects in the region.</p><p><strong>Chaqchan Mosque</strong> in the old town dates from the fourteenth century and is one of the oldest mosques in Baltistan, with carved wooden architecture that shows the Tibetan and Kashmiri influences that run through Balti building.</p><p><strong>The Shyok valley</strong> itself — orchards, terraced fields, poplar avenues and villages, best explored slowly on foot or by vehicle with stops.</p><p><strong>The Hushe road</strong> climbs from Khaplu toward Masherbrum, Laila Peak and the Gondogoro glacier, past Kanday where the Nangma valley opens. Even a day trip up this road gives a sense of the high Karakoram without any trekking.</p><h2><strong>How long to stay</strong></h2><p>One night is enough to see the palace and the mosque. <strong>Two or three nights</strong> allows the Hushe road, a walk in the villages and time at the palace itself, which is a destination rather than just a hotel. Khaplu combines naturally with Shigar and Deosai, as in our <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/tours/deosai-khaplu-tour/">Deosai and Khaplu tour</a>.</p><h2><strong>When to go</strong></h2><p>May to October gives pleasant weather and open roads. April brings blossom to the orchards; late September and October bring autumn colour, which in Khaplu is particularly good against the bare rock. Winter is cold and quiet, and the Hushe road becomes difficult.</p><h2><strong>Where to stay</strong></h2><p>Khaplu Palace is the best-known option and worth a night for its own sake. There are also guest houses and simpler hotels in the town, and basic accommodation in Hushe for those continuing up the valley.</p><h2><strong>Practical notes</strong></h2><p>Mobile coverage is reasonable in Khaplu and fades up the Hushe road. There is no ATM, so bring cash from Skardu. Ghanche is a conservative district, slightly more so than Skardu or Hunza, and loose, modest clothing is appreciated in the villages. Ask before photographing people, particularly women.</p><h2><strong>Journeys from Khaplu</strong></h2><p>The <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/trekking/thalle-la-trek/">Thalle La trek</a> finishes here after crossing from Shigar, and the <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/trekking/nangma-valley-trek/">Nangma valley trek</a> starts from Kanday up the Hushe road. Climbers heading for <a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/expeditions/laila-peak-expedition/">Laila Peak</a> pass through Khaplu on the way in.</p><h2><strong>Related journeys</strong></h2><ul><li><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/tours/deosai-khaplu-tour/">Deosai and Khaplu tour</a></p></li><li><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/jeep-safaris/shigar-basho-khaplu-jeep-safari/">Shigar and Khaplu jeep safari</a></p></li><li><p><a target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" href="/trekking/thalle-la-trek/">Thalle La trek</a></p></li></ul><p></p>
