<h2><strong>When a Coaster is the right vehicle</strong></h2><p>There is a point at which splitting a party across three or four cars stops making sense. Convoys separate on long days, the group eats in shifts, nobody hears the same thing from the guide, and the cost of four drivers exceeds the cost of one bus. A Coaster solves all of that in a single decision.</p><p>It seats a full tour group, a university party, a corporate team or an extended family travelling together, typically in the twenty-plus range depending on the vehicle and how much luggage is going with it. On the long highway days between Islamabad, Gilgit, Hunza and Skardu it is usually both the cheaper option and the more sociable one.</p><h2><strong>Where it goes, and where it stops</strong></h2><p>A Coaster is a highway vehicle. It is comfortable on the Karakoram Highway and on the sealed valley roads, and it is the wrong vehicle the moment the tarmac ends.</p><p>Deosai, Basho, Hushe, the Askole track and most of the high side valleys cannot take a bus of this size &mdash; not because of the driver but because of the turning circle, the overhangs and the river crossings. For those days we move the group into jeeps and bring the Coaster round to meet them, and the whole arrangement sits inside one booking and one quotation rather than two.</p><h2><strong>The driver</strong></h2><p>Every Coaster comes with its driver. These are men who have run the Karakoram Highway for years and who know where the road habitually slides, where the fuel is, and how long a section really takes as opposed to how long the map suggests. Self-drive hire is not offered, and we would not recommend it on these roads to anyone who has not driven them before.</p><p>Driver duty hours are real and we plan around them. A fourteen-hour day with a tired man at the wheel on the Indus gorge road is not a saving.</p><h2><strong>Booking and cost</strong></h2><p>Hired by the day or for a complete itinerary. Fuel, the driver allowance, tolls and any night the vehicle spends away from base are set out in the written quotation before you commit, so there is nothing to negotiate at a roadside.</p><p>Large vehicles are the first thing to go in the summer season &mdash; July and August are booked out well in advance by domestic groups &mdash; so send us your dates as early as you can fix them.</p><p>Availability, driver duty hours, luggage space, fuel, road permissions and overnight charges are confirmed in the written transport quotation.</p><h2><strong>Related</strong></h2><ul><li><p><a href="/vehicles/toyota-hiace-van/">Toyota Hiace van</a> &mdash; for eight to twelve people on the same roads</p></li><li><p><a href="/vehicles/toyota-prado-4x4/">Toyota Prado 4x4</a> &mdash; for the valleys a Coaster cannot enter</p></li><li><p><a href="/plan-a-trip/">Plan a group trip</a> &mdash; tell us the numbers and we will work out the combination</p></li></ul>
