<h2><strong>The default vehicle for families and small groups</strong></h2><p>Most of our guests travel in a Hiace, and for a group of eight to twelve it is almost always the right answer. It seats the party together with room behind for suitcases, camera bags and trekking duffles, which a car cannot do and a Coaster does at a cost nobody needs to pay.</p><p>On the long highway stretches &mdash; Islamabad to Gilgit, Gilgit to Hunza, the Skardu road &mdash; it is comfortable enough for a ten-hour day, and small enough to stop where a bus could not: a viewpoint, a roadside tea shop, a bridge somebody wants to photograph.</p><h2><strong>Airport transfers and short hops</strong></h2><p>A Hiace is also what meets most arrivals. Islamabad airport to a city hotel at three in the morning, Skardu airport to the hotel on a flight day, a half-day around the Hunza forts &mdash; these are hired by the transfer or by the day rather than as part of a full itinerary, and they are quoted the same way.</p><h2><strong>Luggage, and being honest about it</strong></h2><p>The seat count and the luggage count are not the same number. Twelve people with twelve trekking duffles and twelve daypacks do not fit in one Hiace, whatever the brochure seating says, and a vehicle packed to the roof is uncomfortable for a ten-hour day.</p><p>Tell us the group size <em>and</em> what each person is carrying. For expedition teams with heavy kit we add a cargo jeep or a second vehicle and quote the pair together. It is better to find this out at the quotation stage than in a hotel car park at six in the morning.</p><h2><strong>Where it does not go</strong></h2><p>Sealed roads and main valley routes, yes. Deosai, Basho, Hushe and the rough tracks, no. For those sections the group transfers into jeeps and we arrange it inside the same booking, so you are not making two arrangements with two suppliers.</p><h2><strong>Driver and quotation</strong></h2><p>A driver who knows the highway and the mountain sections comes with the vehicle; self-drive hire is not offered. Seat count, luggage capacity, driver hours, fuel, tolls and any overnight allowance are set out in writing before anything is confirmed.</p><h2><strong>Related</strong></h2><ul><li><p><a href="/vehicles/toyota-prado-4x4/">Toyota Prado 4x4</a> &mdash; four to five people and the rough valleys</p></li><li><p><a href="/tours/gilgit-baltistan-grand-circuit/">Gilgit-Baltistan grand circuit</a> &mdash; a long road itinerary this vehicle suits</p></li></ul>
