<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2>
<p>Hunza is the valley that made northern Pakistan famous - terraced fields on impossible slopes, thousand-year-old forts above the river, and Rakaposhi filling the view from the breakfast table. This ten-day journey works its way up from Gilgit through Nagar into Hunza, with time at Baltit and Altit forts in Karimabad, at Attabad Lake, and out to the Passu Cones and the upper valley.</p>
<p><strong>Who it suits.</strong> Travellers who want culture and scenery rather than trekking. There is no walking beyond easy village strolls, the roads are good by mountain standards, and the pace leaves time to sit with a pot of tea and watch the valley.</p>
<p><strong>When to go.</strong> Late March to mid-April for blossom, May to September for warm clear days, and mid-October to early November for autumn colour.</p>
<p><strong>What we handle.</strong> Private vehicle and driver, hotels, a local guide, and the route planning along the Karakoram Highway. Groups of two to twelve work comfortably, and the dates are yours.</p>
