<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2>
<p>Fairy Meadows gives the most accessible close view of an 8,000m mountain anywhere in Pakistan. From the Karakoram Highway a jeep climbs the famous track from Raikot Bridge to Tato, and a walk of two to three hours through pine forest opens onto the meadow with the Raikot face of Nanga Parbat directly ahead.</p>
<p><strong>What the journey covers.</strong> Eight days from Islamabad along the Karakoram Highway, with nights at Fairy Meadows and time to walk on toward Beyal and the viewpoint above the base camp area.</p>
<p><strong>Who it suits.</strong> Travellers who want a mountain experience without a trek, and photographers after the Raikot face at sunrise. The only walking required is the climb to the meadows, and porters or ponies can carry bags.</p>
<p><strong>When to go.</strong> The jeep road is generally open May to October; June to September gives the clearest views.</p>
<p><strong>What we handle.</strong> Transport along the Karakoram Highway, the local jeep to Tato, huts or tented camp at Fairy Meadows, meals and a guide. Accommodation up there is simple and cold at night - the view from the door is the reason people come.</p>
