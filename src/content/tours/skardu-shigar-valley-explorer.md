<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2><p>Skardu is where the Indus turns and the Karakoram begins. Within an hour of the town there are turquoise lakes, a cold desert of sand dunes between the mountains, a fort-palace in the Shigar valley, and the road that leads to Askole where every Baltoro expedition starts. This seven-day journey uses Skardu as a base and goes out in a different direction each day.</p><p><strong>What the journey covers.</strong> Shangrila and Upper Kachura lakes, Shigar fort and valley, the Sarfaranga cold desert, Katpana dunes, and viewpoints along the Indus, with free days to add what interests you.</p><p><strong>Who it suits.</strong> Travellers with about a week, families, and anyone who prefers one comfortable base to a new hotel every night. No trekking is required.</p><p><strong>When to go.</strong> April to October. Spring brings blossom, summer opens every side road, and October brings autumn colour.</p><p><strong>What we handle.</strong> Hotels in Skardu and Shigar, private transport with driver, a local guide, and flights or road travel from Islamabad - planned with a contingency, because the Skardu flight depends on weather.</p><p>Karakoram Vision Pakistan plans this programme around confirmed road access, accommodation, weather and local operating conditions.</p><p><strong>Program</strong></p><h2><strong>Planning notes</strong></h2><p>This is a starter itinerary rather than a binding quotation.
