<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2>
<p>In the first weeks of spring the valleys of Gilgit-Baltistan fill with apricot and cherry blossom while the peaks above are still deep in winter snow. It is a short season and an extraordinary one - white and pink orchards against bare rock and ice, village festivals, and roads still quiet before the summer crowds.</p>
<p><strong>What the journey covers.</strong> Sixteen unhurried days through Gilgit-Baltistan, following the bloom as it moves up the valleys, with time in Hunza where the orchards are thickest and the viewpoints best.</p>
<p><strong>Who it suits.</strong> Photographers and travellers who want beauty rather than altitude. The walking is easy and optional; this is a journey of valleys, villages and viewpoints.</p>
<p><strong>When to go.</strong> Usually late March to mid-April. Blossom starts lower and moves upward, so the dates shift by a week or two each year with the winter - we advise once you tell us when you can travel.</p>
<p><strong>What we handle.</strong> Hotels, private transport with driver, a local guide, and a flexible plan that can stop wherever the orchards are at their best. Spring weather is changeable, so road conditions are checked daily.</p>
