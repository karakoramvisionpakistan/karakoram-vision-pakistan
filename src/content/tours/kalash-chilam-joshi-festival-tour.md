<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2>
<p>The Kalash are a small community living in three valleys of Chitral, with their own language, religion and traditions. Chilam Joshi is their spring festival, held each May across Bumburet, Rumbur and Birir over several days, with dancing, singing and the blessing of the herds before they move to summer pastures.</p>
<h2><strong>Dates</strong></h2>
<p>The Kalash set the festival dates themselves each year, usually in mid-May. We confirm them before you book flights, and the itinerary moves with them.</p>
<h2><strong>Being a good guest</strong></h2>
<p>The festival is a living celebration, not a show. Ask before photographing anyone, especially women, and follow your guide's lead on where to stand and when to step back.</p>
