<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2>
<p>Every July, teams from Gilgit and Chitral meet on the Shandur Pass, at about 3,700 metres, for a polo tournament first held here in 1936. The game is freestyle mountain polo, fast and rough, with music, dancing and a tented camp around the ground.</p>
<h2><strong>Dates</strong></h2>
<p>The festival dates are set each year by the governments of Gilgit-Baltistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, usually in the first half of July. We confirm them before you book flights.</p>
<h2><strong>The route</strong></h2>
<p>The tour goes in from Gilgit through the Ghizer valley and comes out through Chitral, so you cross the pass from one side to the other, with a day in the Kalash valleys.</p>
