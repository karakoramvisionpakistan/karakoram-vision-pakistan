<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2>
<p>For about a thousand years the region of Gandhara, around Taxila, Peshawar and Swat, was one of the great centres of Buddhism, and the place where Greek and Indian art met to create the first images of the Buddha in human form. This tour visits its museums, monasteries and stupas.</p>
<h2><strong>The sites</strong></h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Taxila:</strong> the museum and the monasteries of Jaulian, Sirkap and Dharmarajika</li>
<li><strong>Peshawar Museum:</strong> one of the largest collections of Gandhara art</li>
<li><strong>Takht-i-Bahi:</strong> a hilltop monastery and UNESCO World Heritage Site</li>
<li><strong>Swat:</strong> Butkara, Shingardar and Saidu stupas, the Swat Museum and the rock-carved Jehanabad Buddha</li>
</ul>
