<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2>
<p>This is the journey for travellers who want the whole region rather than one valley. Fourteen days linking Gilgit, Hunza, Skardu, Shigar and Khaplu: the Karakoram Highway and the Hunza forts on one side, the Indus valley, the cold desert and the Balti palaces on the other, with the mountains changing character as you cross between them.</p>
<p><strong>Who it suits.</strong> First-time visitors with time, and travellers who would rather see the contrast between Hunza and Baltistan than choose one. Nothing requires trekking; the days are a mix of driving, sightseeing and short walks.</p>
<p><strong>When to go.</strong> May to October. Summer opens every road including Deosai; October brings autumn colour and clear air.</p>
<p><strong>What we handle.</strong> Hotels throughout, a private vehicle with an experienced mountain driver, a local guide, and the route planning that keeps driving days to a sensible length. Fourteen days is the shortest comfortable version of this circuit - shorter means long days behind glass, longer means free days in the valleys you like most.</p>
