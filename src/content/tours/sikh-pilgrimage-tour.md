<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2>
<p>Pakistan holds many of the most sacred places in Sikhism. This tour visits the birthplace of Guru Nanak at Nankana Sahib, Gurdwara Darbar Sahib at Kartarpur where he spent his final years, Gurdwara Panja Sahib at Hasan Abdal with his handprint on the rock, and the gurdwaras of Lahore.</p>
<h2><strong>Timing</strong></h2>
<p>The tour runs all year. Vaisakhi and the birthday of Guru Nanak are the busiest times at the gurdwaras; tell us if you want to be there for either.</p>
