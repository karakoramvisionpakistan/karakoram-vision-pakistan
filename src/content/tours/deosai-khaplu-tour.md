faqs:
  - question: When is Deosai open?
    answer: >-
      Usually from late May or June until late September or October, depending on snow. Outside that
      the plateau roads are closed and the tour is planned without them.
  - question: Can you see brown bears on Deosai?
    answer: >-
      Sometimes. The Himalayan brown bear lives on the plateau and sightings happen, most often
      early morning or evening, but no one can promise them. Marmots, foxes and birdlife are near
      certain.
  - question: How high is the Deosai plateau?
    answer: >-
      Around 4,000m, which is high enough that some travellers feel the altitude. The itinerary
      reaches it after time in Skardu so you arrive partly acclimatised.
  - question: What is there to see in Khaplu?
    answer: >-
      The restored Khaplu Palace, the old mosque and wooden village architecture, and the Shyok
      valley road with views toward the Hushe peaks.

<h2><strong>About this journey</strong></h2><p>Deosai is the second highest plateau in the world, an ocean of grass and wildflowers at around 4,000m where the horizon is wider than anything else in Pakistan and the Himalayan brown bear still lives. Khaplu, on the other side of this journey, is its opposite: a green valley of apricot orchards below the restored palace, with the Shyok river running toward the Hushe peaks.</p><p><strong>What the journey covers.</strong> Eight days from Skardu, taking in Sheosar Lake and the plateau crossing, the Shigar valley, and Khaplu with its palace and old mosque.</p><p><strong>Who it suits.</strong> Travellers who want wilderness and heritage in one trip without trekking. The plateau is high, so the itinerary reaches it after time in Skardu to allow some adjustment.</p><p><strong>When to go.</strong> Late May or June until around late September, depending entirely on snow - Deosai can only be crossed when the road is officially open.</p><p><strong>What we handle.</strong> 4x4 transport with a driver who knows the plateau tracks, hotels, meals and a local guide.</p><p>Karakoram Vision Pakistan plans this programme around confirmed road access, accommodation, weather and local operating conditions.</p><h2><strong>Program</strong></h2><h3><strong>Day 1: Arrival in Skardu</strong></h3><p>Meet the team and review the road and weather plan.</p><h3><strong>Day 2: Drive to Deosai</strong></h3><p>Enter the high plateau via Satpara and camp or lodge according to the selected package.</p><h3><strong>Day 3: Deosai exploration</strong></h3><p>Visit suitable viewpoints and wildlife observation areas without disturbing habitats.</p><h3><strong>Day 4: Return to Skardu</strong></h3><p>Drive back with scenic stops.</p><h3><strong>Day 5: Shigar heritage</strong></h3><p>Visit Shigar Fort and the traditional settlement.</p><h3><strong>Day 6: Drive to Khaplu</strong></h3><p>Follow the Shyok Valley to Khaplu.</p><h3><strong>Day 7: Khaplu day</strong></h3><p>Visit Khaplu Palace, Chaqchan Mosque and nearby villages.</p><h3><strong>Day 8: Drop to the Skardu airport</strong></h3><p>Return or continue according to the confirmed transport plan.</p><h2><strong>Planning notes</strong></h2><p>This is a starter itinerary rather than a binding quotation.
